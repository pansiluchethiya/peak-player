title: Legal and responsible use
description: Content responsibility, privacy expectations, and deployment obligations.
---

Peak is playback infrastructure. It does not grant rights to any movie, series, channel, or other
media. Whoever deploys or uses Peak is responsible for the content they access and for complying
with applicable law, contracts, and platform policies.

## Content responsibility

- Only use sources and media you are authorized to access.
- Respect provider terms of service, rate limits, and geographic restrictions.
- Do not use Peak to bypass paywalls, DRM, or access controls.
- Do not redistribute credentials, cookies, or provider URLs.
- Remove content promptly when you no longer have the right to use it.

## Provider terms

Peak aggregates third-party providers. Provider availability and terms can change without notice.
You are responsible for reviewing the terms that apply to your deployment and for configuring only
providers you are permitted to use.

## Privacy

Peak can process viewer IP addresses, user agents, playback events, and approximate performance
data. A production deployment should:

- Publish a privacy notice describing what is collected and why.
- Minimize retention of viewer identifiers.
- Redact tokens, API keys, and upstream URLs from logs.
- Provide a contact path for privacy requests.
- Apply appropriate cross-border transfer safeguards.

The embed SDK itself does not collect analytics. Telemetry is controlled by the player and your
deployment configuration.

## Security

Do not publicly disclose live tokens, partner keys, or exploitable details. Report security issues
privately to the deployment owner. See [Security](/docs/security) for the threat model.

## No warranty

The software is provided as-is, without warranties of availability, fitness for a particular
purpose, or non-infringement. You are responsible for backups, monitoring, incident response, and
compliance.

## Copyright complaints

If you operate a public deployment, provide a documented process for copyright complaints that
includes:

1. A contact address.
2. The specific content and URL at issue.
3. A statement of authorization.
4. A process for removal and confirmation.

Do not publish a real complaint address in this documentation; configure one for your deployment.

## Changes

These notes are not legal advice. Review them with counsel for your jurisdiction and use case.
