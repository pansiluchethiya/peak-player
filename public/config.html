How-to

Find your cookie

Six steps in a desktop browser.

1Open FebBox

Go to febbox.com in your browser.

2Sign in with Google

For safety, use a fresh account that you do not use for anything else.

3Open Developer Tools

Press F12, or right-click the page and choose Inspect.

4Navigate to cookies

Open the Application tab, expand Storage, and select Cookies.

5Find the ui cookie

Look for a cookie named exactly ui.

6Copy and add

Copy only its value, paste it above, and choose Add cookie. Save overrides when you are done.
